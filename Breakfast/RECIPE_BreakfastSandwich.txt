1 English Muffin - Sliced in half
1 Egg - Fried 
1 Slice of Cheese
1 Breakfast Sausage Patty

Fry the egg in a circular mold to make it fit inside the muffin. using the same pan cook the sausage and the muffin to make them crisp

assemble by first placing the patty, the cheese, then the egg, then the last piece of the English muffin.
