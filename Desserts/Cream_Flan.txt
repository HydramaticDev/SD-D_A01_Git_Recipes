Ingredients:
Flan
1 can of Evaporated Milk (354 ML)
1 Box of All Purpose Cream
(Substitute with 1 cup of Heavy Whipping Cream)
106 ml of Water
(Combine these 3 to have 3 cups)
1/2 cup white sugar
1/4 tsp salt
4 Egg Yolks
2 Whole Eggs

Caramel
1/4 Cup of Sugar
pinch of salt

Directions:

Preheat Oven to 325 Degrees F
Place an oven safe container and fill half way with water.

Make a caramel in the stove with the sugar and salt and pour into oven-safe containers.
(We filipinos use a Lanera)
Then set aside

in a bowl mix together the Milk, Cream and Water
then in another bowl carefully mix the eggs, egg yolk, sugar, and salt to prevent air bubbles.
Then combine the milk and egg mixture and add the vanilla.
Mix slowly to prevent air bubbles.

Run the mixture through a strainer to make it silky smooth.

Add the custard mixture into the oven-safe containers/Laneras and then cover with aluminum foil

Carefully place into the Water Bath and bake for 40 minutes.

Once cooked place inside fridge to set. About an hour.
