270 grams of warm milk
35 grams melted butter
12 grams active dry yeast
265 grams all purpose flour
195 grams whole wheat flour
110 grams white sugar
60 grams of softened salted butter.

Filling:
120 grams of softened salted butter
160 grams of dark brown sugar
16 grams of ground cinnamon
10 grams of Vanilla Extract

Method:
Mix together warm milk and melted butter into a container, add the egg and egg yolk and mix thoroughly, and then mix in the yeast and let bloom. In a separate bowl mix together the flours, and sugar until thoroughly combined. Combine the wet and dry ingredients into the bowl of a stand mixer. Knead the dough at low speed for 12 minutes. 9 minutes in slowly add the softened butter that was set aside for the dough, keep kneading until the dough is soft and somewhat smooth, but still slightly sticky. Transfer to a buttered bowl and let it rise for 1.5 hours in a warm environment. In a separate bowl mix together the butter, sugar, and cinnamon until thoroughly combined and light brown on colour. Once the dough has fully proofed, punch the dough down and make sure it’s fully deflated. On a lightly floured surface, roll the dough into a large rectangular sheet 38X28Cm. Spread the fillings and roll the longest side. Cut the dough into 12 equal rolls and place into a baking dish. Place the rolls into a warm environment for 45 minutes. While the rolls are proofing, preheat the oven to 190C. Once the oven is done preheating and the rolls have increased in size, place the baking dish into the oven for 20 minutes.
